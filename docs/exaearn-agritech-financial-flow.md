# ExaEarn AgriTech Financial Flow

## Participation

An idempotency key is mandatory. The service validates ownership-independent server inputs, compliance, security, project eligibility, central pricing policy, and share capacity under a database row lock. Funds are reserved from the user's canonical funding account and consumed by a balanced settlement into the segregated AgriTech investor escrow account. Only then is allocation confirmed.

## Disbursement

Disbursement requires an approved project milestone, approved farmer, escrow coverage, maker approval and a different checker. Settlement moves value from investor escrow to the farmer's canonical funding account.

## Revenue and payout

Harvest revenue requires approved source evidence. Verified revenue is credited from the AgriTech settlement account; verified costs and applicable central pricing fees are recorded separately. Payout allocations use financially settled shares and canonical balanced entries.

## Refund

Only eligible cancelled, failed or refunding projects may refund. The operation verifies escrow coverage, posts one idempotent ledger transaction and releases the associated share allocation.
