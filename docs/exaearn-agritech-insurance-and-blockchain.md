# ExaEarn AgriTech Insurance and Blockchain Policy

Insurance is not represented as active unless a real policy/provider record exists. Coverage, exclusions, claim state and insurer obligations remain provider-controlled external facts.

Blockchain references may support provenance after approval, but they do not replace land registry searches, legal title, identity review, jurisdiction policy, custody analysis or securities classification. Tokenized investment is disabled by default and cannot be enabled by frontend input.
