# ExaEarn Phase 17 Chart of Accounts

The initial chart of accounts is seeded by `FinanceAccountingService`.

Top-level groups:
- 1000 Assets
- 2000 Liabilities
- 3000 Equity
- 4000 Revenue
- 5000 Expenses
- 9000 Suspense

Mappings from canonical ledger account types to finance accounts are stored in `finance_account_mappings` and versioned by policy version.

The chart is configurable and should be reviewed by professional accounting advisors before formal statutory reporting.
