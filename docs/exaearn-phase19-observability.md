# ExaEarn Phase 19 Observability

Phase 19 adds persistent health and operational evidence:

- service registry
- dependency checks
- health snapshots
- operational alerts with deduplication, acknowledgement and resolution
- SLO definitions and error-budget policies
- queue states
- worker heartbeats
- backup records
- recovery actions

Production observability drains, dashboards, paging and retention remain OPERATIONAL SETUP REQUIRED until connected in the deployment environment.
