# ExaEarn AgriTech Operations

The existing admin application and `agri.manage` permission remain authoritative. Operations APIs provide summary, evidence review, project transitions, milestones, maker-checker disbursement, reconciliation and refunds.

Recommended production sequence:

1. Keep public funding disabled.
2. Configure jurisdiction and product policy.
3. Establish identity, farm, land and revenue verification operations.
4. Complete legal classification and provider due diligence.
5. Fund and reconcile segregated accounts.
6. Enable one approved project as a limited rollout.
7. Reconcile every milestone and settlement before expansion.

Mobile currently provides product discovery only; full project, evidence and investment flows remain a future mobile migration.
