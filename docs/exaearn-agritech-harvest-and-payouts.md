# ExaEarn AgriTech Harvest Settlement and Investor Payouts

Produce reports are operational observations and remain unverified on submission. A harvest settlement requires separately approved `HARVEST_REVENUE` evidence, source type, external reference, accounting period, asset and idempotency key.

Distributable value is derived with deterministic decimal arithmetic from verified revenue less verified costs and applicable central pricing decisions. Allocation is proportional to financially settled shares. Each investor payout has its own stable ledger reference, making retries economically idempotent.

Returns are variable and may be zero or negative. The software does not promise yield, fabricate revenue, or treat deposits as harvest income.
