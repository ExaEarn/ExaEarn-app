# ExaEarn Phase 15A Listing Lifecycle

The lifecycle is:

1. Applicant draft.
2. Applicant submission.
3. Internal reviews.
4. Maker recommendation.
5. Checker approval.
6. Asset configuration.
7. Market configuration.
8. Listing test run.
9. Final approval request.
10. Checker schedule approval.
11. Launch-time revalidation.
12. Live trading activation.

Deposits and withdrawals remain separate operational controls and are not implicitly opened by the trading launch.
