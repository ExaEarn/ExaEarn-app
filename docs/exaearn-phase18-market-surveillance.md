# ExaEarn Phase 18 Market Surveillance

`MarketSurveillanceService` creates central market-integrity signals and cases for:

- self trade
- related-account trading
- wash trading
- spoofing/layering
- quote stuffing
- copy trading manipulation
- MM/MM Bot abuse
- OTC anomalies

It does not fabricate guilt or automatically punish on one heuristic. It creates reviewable security cases.
