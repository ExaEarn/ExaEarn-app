# ExaEarn Phase 15F Institutional Risk

`InstitutionalRiskOverviewService` consolidates cross-product risk visibility for active institutions.

## Current Coverage

- Active institutional accounts.
- Subaccount count.
- Market-maker bot count.
- Open OTC trade count.
- Open futures position count.
- Market-maker bot cross-product exposure.
- Treasury boundary categories.

## Boundaries

The overview does not mutate balances, positions, orders or trades. Authoritative financial state remains in the OMS, ledger, reservations, settlement and product-specific services.
