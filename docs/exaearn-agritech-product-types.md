# ExaEarn AgriTech Product Types

Supported economic classifications are configuration and policy inputs, not legal conclusions: `PROJECT_REGISTRY`, `DONATION`, `PRE_PURCHASE`, `INVESTMENT`, `REVENUE_SHARE`, and `TOKENIZED_INVESTMENT` where enabled.

Investment, revenue-share and tokenized products require explicit legal approval. `TOKENIZED_INVESTMENT` additionally requires the tokenization feature flag. A database record, share allocation or blockchain reference does not itself confer land ownership, liquidity, guaranteed yield, or regulatory approval.

Default deployment mode is sandbox with public investment disabled.
