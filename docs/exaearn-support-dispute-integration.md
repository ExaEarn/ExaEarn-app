# ExaEarn Support Dispute Integration

Support tickets can reference product records with `related_entity_type` and `related_entity_id`.

Current unified dispute operations view surfaces:

- P2P disputes
- ExaCard disputes

ExaPay, Giftcard, Staking, Security, Compliance and Finance remain linked through ticket metadata/entity references until deeper product-specific agent panels are added. Support does not duplicate product dispute state and cannot settle disputes outside the authoritative product workflow.
