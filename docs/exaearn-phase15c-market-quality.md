# ExaEarn Phase 15C Market Quality

Market quality snapshots are derived from active Phase 8 market-maker quote records and market assignments.

## Metrics

- best bid
- best ask
- spread in basis points
- bid depth
- ask depth
- quote presence
- market maker count
- health score

## Policy

External exchange depth is not mixed into ExaEarn internal market-maker quality calculations. External venues can be reported separately through the Phase 8 liquidity source registry.
