# ExaSkills Business Training

Business training is built on the existing ExaEarn identity model.

Implemented:

- business organizations
- owner/admin/manager/learner members
- email invitations
- seat allocation
- training programs
- course assignments
- assignment-backed course entitlement
- business dashboard reporting for members, seats, assignments and programs

Invoice-based enterprise billing is an operational setup item; subscription-based business billing is supported by the same ExaSkills subscription architecture.
